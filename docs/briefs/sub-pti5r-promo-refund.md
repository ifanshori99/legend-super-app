## Bug

`refileRecoveryRefundAfterFailure` (`services/order-recovery-refund.ts` ~872) has a closure `settleFinalFailure` (~919) that stamps `order_issues.refund_rejected_at` and requests the journal reversal. The first filing (~636) recorded the goods payout on the promo ledger: `orderPromoPartialRefundPort.recordPartialRefundPayout` adds to `promotion_order_applications.refunded_paid_idr`, and `promoRecordedIdr` is stored in the first filing's `payment_admin_idempotency.response` (key `orderRecoveryRefundKey(issueId)` = `order-recovery-refund:<issueId>`). The refund then fails finally (`provider_declined`, `cap_exceeded`, `source_document`, `refile_limit`), the line is re-reported as a NEW issue, and the recompute (`crm-promo-refund.ts` ~690) computes `refundDueIdr` ~0 because `refunded_paid_idr` still includes the first payout -> `nothing_left` -> guest money stuck.

(2) re-report recompute has refundedNow=0 (refundedSubtotalIdr still counts the line) -> `order.refunded` gross=net, discount=0 (books wrong; `order-recovery-refund-amount.ts` promoParts ~188). ## Round 3 (CTO decision after architect review of 327e0135) — REPLACES the give-back

Why: a promo withdrawn by the first filing (voucher re-issued, usage decremented) cannot be undone, so treating the line as kept after a give-back made a later different item (e.g. air Rp5.000) end `nothing_left`. Decision: **the promo ledger is not touched on a final failure.** The payout stays reserved for that line (`refunded_paid_idr`, `refunded_subtotal_idr` unchanged), so no other issue can absorb it. When the SAME item is re-reported after a non-`rejected` final failure, the new issue re-files the failed refund's amount + split, without promo recompute and without `recordPartialRefundPayout`, capped by the intent's current refundable room. `rejected` unchanged (re-report -> nothing_left, legend-h1dyy).

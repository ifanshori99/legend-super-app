Volumes, WAL churn, network and workloads require measurement. USD conversion is a **planning assumption Rp16,000/USD**, not a quoted exchange rate. Prices exclude taxes, FX/transfer fees, domain, engineering, monitoring, networking overages, edge hardware, WAN, staff, payment MDR and vendor setup unless noted. ## Residency, reliability and implementation gates

Production app and PostgreSQL must run in Jakarta and live S3 objects must be Jakarta-resident per PRD. Confirm geographic placement in signed terms for the exact bucket and replicas; cloud CDN, email, WhatsApp, AI and maps can process data internationally. Minimize personal data and transfer only with an appropriate DPA, documented subprocessors and a valid legal basis under [UU 27/2022, especially Article 56](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022); obtain legal review of cross-border safeguards, not an assumption that a DPA alone suffices. AI vendors must not train on customer data. Retention and deletion follow PRD §2, including 10-year financial records, 2-year audit/access logs and deletion/anonymization requirements. Encrypt sensitive fields at application level, encrypt backup and in transit, isolate tenant objects, rotate keys, and avoid personal data in logs. CDN must not cache private data.

Cloud WAL archiving must actually meet RPO ≤5 minutes, with a separate-location backup and a demonstrated RTO ≤4 hours. Test full restore and point-in-time recovery before release and quarterly; monitor WAL freshness and restore integrity. Edge snapshots every 15 minutes plus continuous sync and standby activation ≤15 minutes remain separate requirements. Price/location alone prove none of these. Exercise real one-outlet peak load and model 50 outlets before sizing or promising the 99.9% target. Keep adapters fake-driven while procurement is pending, with contract tests for retries, idempotency, isolation and error handling.

Wi-Fi controller, smart lock and delivery platforms are not selected by this bead. Preserve PRD `WifiProvider`, `LockProvider`, and `DeliveryChannel` boundaries and fake implementations; vendor selection and bank/platform approvals are dependencies, not assumptions. Wi-Fi and lock choice has the PRD deadline of 2 November 2026. Guests use mobile data for reverse OTP during outlet WAN failure; cashiers can issue offline Wi-Fi vouchers.

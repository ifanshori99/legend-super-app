## Reverse OTP feasibility and AUTH-02 gap

KirimDev [documents inbound messages](https://docs.kirimdev.com/sending/receive-messages/) as signed `message.received` webhook POSTs with the sender, message text and receiving `phone_number_id`. Thus **inbound-message reverse-OTP login is technically supported**, subject to integration and live tests; this is not a claim that login has been implemented or verified. Route the receiving number to the correct tenant, deduplicate the Meta message ID, and bind the one-use, short-lived challenge to its requesting browser. Default to no WhatsApp reply (AUTH-03); inbound is customer initiated, and any enabled replies/templates can incur separate Meta charges. Starter's 200,000 messages/month is a platform allowance, not proof of free Meta billing; number add-ons and taxes are extra. [KirimDev pricing](https://kirimdev.com/blog/paket-harga-kirimdev/), [billing](https://docs.kirimdev.com/guides/whatsapp-business-payment/).

**Release blocker:** PRD AUTH-02 explicitly requires `X-Hub-Signature-256`. KirimDev's [webhook contract](https://docs.kirimdev.com/webhooks/overview/) documents `X-Kirim-Signature` HMAC over its delivery, not a verified original Meta signature. A Meta-shaped body does not prove preservation of the original signature. Do not treat the Kirim signature as the required Meta signature or disable verification. Obtain documented preservation of the original signature and exact signed bytes, or an approved PRD/security change accepting KirimDev's authenticated delivery; test forgery, replay, rotation, duplicate IDs and routing before launch. Follow [KirimDev signature verification](https://docs.kirimdev.com/webhooks/overview/) and its delivery/retry contract. No actual provider account or end-to-end login was tested here.

## Data and procurement gates

PRD §2 requires Jakarta app/database and live objects; verify exact physical placement and backup separation in writing. CDN, email, WhatsApp and AI may process data elsewhere: minimize personal data and obtain legal review of cross-border transfers under [UU 27/2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022), applicable DPA and subprocessors. Preserve PRD retention (including ten-year financial and two-year audit/access records), encryption, key rotation and deletion. No price or free tier proves availability or data residency. Wi-Fi/lock selection, EDC approval, email eligibility and real vendor tests remain open.

**Production outbound adapter (legend-w8dfz):** `apps/api/src/services/messaging-kirimdev.ts` implements `MessagingProvider.send` against KirimDev's [messages API](https://docs.kirimdev.com/) (`POST /v1/{phone_number_id}/messages`, Bearer `KIRIM_API_KEY`) and webhook verification through `verifyKirimWebhook`. It sends a stable `Idempotency-Key` per message reference, retries 429, 502, 503, `idempotency_in_progress` and network errors (at most 3 attempts), and never retries 4xx such as `outside_24h_window`. It is off unless `KIRIM_API_KEY` is set, and no feature calls it yet. Free text only works within 24 hours of the customer's last message; template messages are out of scope. KirimDev has no sandbox, so tests mock `fetch`; no live account was tested. Messages are billed per the plan and Meta rules above.

## Reverse OTP feasibility and AUTH-02

KirimDev [documents inbound messages](https://docs.kirimdev.com/sending/receive-messages/) as signed `message.received` webhook POSTs with the sender, message text and receiving `phone_number_id`. Thus **inbound-message reverse-OTP login is technically supported**, subject to integration and live tests; this is not a claim that login has been implemented or verified. Route the receiving number to the correct tenant, deduplicate the Meta message ID, and bind the one-use, short-lived challenge to its requesting browser. Default to no WhatsApp reply (AUTH-03); inbound is customer initiated, and any enabled replies/templates can incur separate Meta charges. Starter's 200,000 messages/month is a platform allowance, not proof of free Meta billing; number add-ons and taxes are extra. [KirimDev pricing](https://kirimdev.com/blog/paket-harga-kirimdev/), [billing](https://docs.kirimdev.com/guides/whatsapp-business-payment/).

**Signature scheme (resolved, owner-approved):** PRD AUTH-02 now requires KirimDev's `X-Kirim-Signature` instead of Meta's `X-Hub-Signature-256`. KirimDev's [webhook contract](https://docs.kirimdev.com/webhooks/overview/) signs its own delivery (`t=<unix>,v1=<hex>`, HMAC-SHA256 over `"<t>." + raw body`, 300-second window, one `v1` per active secret during rotation) and sends `X-Kirim-Event-Id` as the dedupe key; it does not preserve Meta's original signature, and the PRD no longer asks for it. The contract is implemented in `packages/shared/src/adapters/webhook.ts` (see `docs/plan/contracts.md`). Forgery, replay, rotation, duplicate-ID and routing tests stay a release gate and must run against a real KirimDev account before launch; verify the exact raw bytes, never re-serialized JSON. No actual provider account or end-to-end login was tested here.

## Data and procurement gates

PRD §2 requires Jakarta app/database and live objects; verify exact physical placement and backup separation in writing. CDN, email, WhatsApp and AI may process data elsewhere: minimize personal data and obtain legal review of cross-border transfers under [UU 27/2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022), applicable DPA and subprocessors. Preserve PRD retention (including ten-year financial and two-year audit/access records), encryption, key rotation and deletion. No price or free tier proves availability or data residency. Wi-Fi/lock selection, EDC approval, email eligibility and real vendor tests remain open.
